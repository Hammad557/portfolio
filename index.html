Project/FYP·AI

FINAL YEAR PROJECT PRESTON UNIVERSITY KOHAT SUPV. MS. LAIBA ZAHOOR AUG 2026

AI-BASED VEHICLE TRACKING, COUNTING & MONITORING SYSTEM FOR TOLL PLAZAS

An end-to-end intelligent traffic monitoring system. Live RTSP streams or recorded MP4 footage flow through a YOLOv8 detection pipeline, get linked into stable trajectories by a Kalman-filter SORT tracker, and are counted with directional virtual gates — all wrapped in a dark-HUD Electron desktop dashboard that an operator can configure without touching a line of code.

The pipeline applies freehand polygonal ROI masking (RDP-simplified), IoU-based duplicate suppression, and per-track centroid line-crossing logic that guarantees each vehicle is counted exactly once — in the right direction.
